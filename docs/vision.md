# Motif — Vision

## What Motif is

> **Motif owns the compounding, intent-rich record of what sparks you, and a
> proactive loop that turns it into things you actually make. The reasoning is
> rented from Claude; the corpus, the intent, and the loop are owned — and they
> get more valuable every month and every model upgrade.**

Motif is not a note app, not a personal wiki, and not "an LLM that answers
questions about my stuff." It is a tool for turning consumption into creation.

## The problem

We consume at a terrifying rate. The things that genuinely spark us get
bookmarked, drafted, screenshotted — and then the spark dies in a graveyard of
tabs and drafts. This is not a *recall* failure (we could find it if we tried).
It is an **action** failure: interest never becomes output. Motif targets that
gap.

Success is not measured in items stored. It is measured in **things made**.

## Why not just an LLM wiki, or "just ask Claude"?

The *intelligence* is commoditized — Claude can already answer "what could I
build from this?" or "what am I into lately?" Motif does not compete with that
and never should. Trying to out-reason a frontier model is a losing bet.

The point is everything that must be true for that query to be *good* and to
*happen at all*. A raw chat has no persistent corpus, no memory of what you
consumed, no captured intent, no initiative, and no place for the output to
live. Those are the things that **compound**.

### Principle: rent the model, own what compounds

A test for every feature:

> **Does a *better* Claude make this feature stronger, or obsolete?**
> Better-model kills it → it was never the moat. Better-model strengthens it →
> build there.

A better model makes Motif's suggestions sharper, its drafts better, its
clustering smarter — while the owned assets (corpus, intent, loop, relationship)
keep compounding. Model progress is a **tailwind**, not a threat.

## The three layers (a stack, not alternatives)

- **A — Consumption → Creation (the mission).** The reason Motif exists: turn
  what sparks you into output — a draft, a project, a thing learned. The
  anti-bookmark.
- **B — Proactive attention loop (the engine).** Motif takes initiative:
  notices patterns in your attention and surfaces the right thread at the right
  moment, unprompted. Push, not pull.
- **C — Curated context layer (the substrate).** The owned, intent-rich,
  provenance-tracked corpus of what catches your eye — the foundation that makes
  A and B possible, and (later) a portable context any agent can plug into.

C is the foundation, B is the engine, A is the outcome. They are one system.

## The moat

**Proprietary, compounding, intent-rich corpus** + **the closed loop
(spark → suggestion → artifact)** + **the initiative to nudge you at the right
moment.**

The model is rented and identical for everyone. The corpus, the captured intent,
the closed loop, and the user habit are owned, unique, and amplified by every
model upgrade.

## How Motif uses Claude

- **Claude is the reasoning engine over the corpus** — clustering sparks into
  themes, proposing things to make, drafting, generating learning paths.
- **The corpus is the context.** Motif's job is to assemble the right slice of
  your intent-rich corpus and hand it to Claude — with prompt caching for the
  stable parts and structured outputs for suggestions.
- **Provider-agnostic, Claude-first.** A thin interface so the model can be
  swapped, but built and tuned for Claude.
- **Later — the "context wallet."** Motif's curated corpus becomes portable
  context any LLM/agent can plug into to be instantly good *about you*. Here
  Motif *feeds* the LLM-wiki world rather than competing with it.

## The risk we must avoid

A **thin wrapper** that just pastes your stuff into Claude is weak and should be
killed. Motif earns the right to exist only if four things are genuinely good:

1. **Capture is frictionless and habitual** — so the corpus is real and current.
2. **Intent is captured at the source** — the *why-this-sparked-me*, the highest-
   value signal, caught before it evaporates.
3. **The loop closes to artifacts** — suggestions become tracked work, not lost
   chat replies.
4. **Motif takes initiative** — the right nudge at the right time.

Get those right and "ask Claude" becomes a feature *inside* Motif, not a
competitor to it.
